Connect ESP32 sensors

Quick start: 5 steps

  1. 1
    Wire the sensors — See wiring diagram below. Connect DHT22 DATA, soil moisture AO, and optional light AO to the ESP32 GPIOs you'll pick in step 3.
  2. 2
    Create a Blynk device — Open Blynk Cloud Console. Create a new device template (or use existing). Note your Template ID and Auth Token.
  3. 3
    Configure & download — Enter Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only), your Blynk Template ID & Auth Token, choose your GPIO pins, click Download sketch.
  4. 4
    Flash to ESP32 — Open the downloaded watermi_blynk.ino in Arduino IDE. Install Blynk library (Sketch → Include Library → Manage Libraries → search "Blynk"). Select board & port. Click Upload. Open Serial Monitor at 115200 baud — you should see encrypted TLS connection status.
  5. 5
    Connect to your plant — Pick your plant below and click Connect Blynk to plant. Live readings appear right here and on your Dashboard automatically!

Wiring diagram

DHT22 VCC → 3.3V DATA → GPIO 4 GND → GND
Soil moisture VCC → 3.3V AO → GPIO 34 GND → GND
Light sensor VCC → 3.3V AO → GPIO 35 GND → GND

⚡ ADC1 pins (32–39) work while Wi-Fi is active. Avoid GPIO 6–11 for DHT22 (strapped pins).
🔌 Bare DHT22: add a 10 kΩ pull-up resistor between DATA and 3.3V.
📶 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only — 5 GHz does not work on ESP32.

Troubleshooting
  • ESP32 won't connect to Wi-Fi: Check 2.4 GHz network name & password
  • "Invalid Auth Token" in Serial Monitor: Re-check Template ID & Auth Token from Blynk Console
  • Blynk shows "Device offline": Check Wi-Fi, restart ESP32, verify Auth Token matches device
  • Temp/humidity shows ERR: Check DHT22 wiring on selected GPIO + 10 kΩ pull-up resistor
  • Soil moisture stuck at 0% or 100%: Calibrate SOIL_DRY_RAW / SOIL_WET_RAW in Advanced options
  • All sensors drop after ~30s: Board stopped — check power supply & Wi-Fi stability
